The rise in pitch is the fingerprint. To leading order, how quickly the frequency f climbs depends only on f itself and on the chirp mass 𝓜 = (m₁m₂)^(3/5)/(m₁ + m₂)^(1/5). Measure f and its rate of change and you have 𝓜 without knowing the distance.

For GW150914 that gave about 30 solar masses, as seen at Earth. Two objects with that much mass orbiting 75 times a second must be within a few hundred kilometres of each other. Neutron stars are too light; ordinary stars would have collided long before. Only black holes fit.
